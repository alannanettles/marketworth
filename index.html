What do I enter?

Booth fee, travel costs, event hours, attendance, booth visit rate, conversion rate, average order value, product gross margin, and optional setup time / extra fixed costs.

What do I get back?

Total event cost, break-even revenue and sales, estimated customers, revenue, gross profit, net profit, profit per hour, and low / expected / high scenarios.

What does gross margin mean?

It’s the percentage of each sale left after the direct cost of making the product. If a $20 item costs $6 in materials, its gross margin is 70%.
