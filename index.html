FAQ

Useful before your next application deadline.

What if I don’t know how many people will visit my booth?

Use a conservative estimate. MarketWorth also shows low and high scenarios so you can see whether the event only works when everything goes perfectly.

What does gross margin mean?

It’s the percentage of each sale left after the direct cost of making the product. If a $20 item costs $6 in materials, its gross margin is 70%.

Does profit per hour include prep time?

Yes, when you enter setup / prep hours. We divide estimated net profit by event hours plus those additional hours.

Is my data saved?

No account or database is used in this version. Calculations happen in your browser. Your inputs stay on your device unless you print or download your own summary.
